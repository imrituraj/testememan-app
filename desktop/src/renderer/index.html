    Warning

    Finding your access code

    • It's in the email from TestMeMan with the subject "Your access code for …".
    • If you registered through your organizer's link, it was shown on screen and emailed to you.
    • Codes have 6 letters and numbers. There is no 0, O, 1 or I, so you can't mix them up.
    • Lost it? Register again with the same email on the organizer's link and we'll resend it.

    Connection lost

    Keep answering. Your answers are stored on this device and sync when you're back online. The timer keeps running.

    Couldn't submit: no internet

    Your answers are saved on this device and the screen is unlocked. Connect to the internet and press Try again, or reopen the app later with your code to submit. If time runs out first, the answers that already reached the server are submitted. Tell your organizer if this keeps happening.
